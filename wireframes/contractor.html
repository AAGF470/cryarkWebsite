Common questions

Good to know

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — we're fully licensed, bonded, and carry general liability and workers' comp insurance. We're happy to provide proof before any work begins.

Do you offer free estimates?

Absolutely. We'll come out, look at the project, and give you a detailed written estimate at no cost or obligation.

How long do projects usually take?

It depends on scope — a bathroom might be 2–3 weeks, a full addition 2–3 months. We give you a firm timeline in your estimate.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We pull all required permits and schedule inspections so everything is fully up to code.
